Thank you for purchasing NEXORA! Follow these quick steps to get started:

1. OPEN THE DOCUMENTATION
   -------------------------------------------------------------------
   Before making changes, open documentation/index.html in any modern
   web browser. It includes a complete reference guide for:
   - File organization and naming conventions
   - Customizing colors and design tokens in CSS
   - Editing products, categories, coupons, and prices in JS
   - Connecting your own backend, payment gateway, or form endpoints
   - Deployment options (Vercel, Netlify, traditional cPanel/Apache/Nginx)

2. PREVIEW THE WEBSITE LOCALLY
   -------------------------------------------------------------------
   To experience the template with all localStorage features active:
   
   Option A (Python 3):
     Open your terminal, navigate to the template/ folder, and run:
     python3 -m http.server 8080
     Then visit http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

   Option B (Node.js):
     Inside the template/ folder, run:
     npx serve .

   Option C (VS Code):
     Install the "Live Server" extension, open template/index.html,
     and click "Go Live" in the bottom status bar.

3. CUSTOMIZATION QUICK REFERENCE
   -------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Global Design Tokens: Open template/assets/css/style.css and
     edit section "02. DESIGN TOKENS" at the top of the file.
   - Products & Store Data: Open template/assets/js/data.js to update
     product titles, prices, descriptions, and coupon codes.
   - Images: Drop your images into template/assets/images/, keeping
     the original dimensions and filenames, or update paths in HTML/JS.

4. IMPORTANT NOTICE
   -------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Cart, wishlist, and checkout are client-side demonstrations.
     Connect your backend payment provider before launching a live store.
   - The preview photography shown in live demos is for display only.
     High-resolution branded placeholders ship in this package.
