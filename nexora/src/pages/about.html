We design objects from the near future.

Founded in 2019 by a small team of industrial designers and audio engineers, NEXORA set out to make technology that feels less like a gadget and more like an heirloom.

Today our studio of 64 people designs, engineers and ships every product in-house — from the first sketch to the final firmware update.

We obsess over the details nobody sees: the tension of a hinge, the latency of a button, the recyclability of a box. Because the future should be built to last.

Our values

Three principles, zero compromises

01 — CRAFT

Considered to the micron

We prototype every product more than 200 times. Materials are chosen for how they age, not just how they look on day one.

02 — HONESTY

Specs you can trust

Battery life, latency and noise-cancelling figures are measured by independent labs and published in full.

03 — LONGEVITY

Built to be repaired

Replaceable batteries, modular parts and five years of software updates for every device we sell.
