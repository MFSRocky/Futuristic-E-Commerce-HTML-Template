{{icon:shopping-bag}}Orders & payment

Add products to your cart, open the cart drawer or cart page and click “Checkout”. Fill in your contact, shipping and payment details and confirm. In this template the checkout is a demonstration interface — no payment is processed.

A production store built on NEXORA would typically accept major credit and debit cards, digital wallets and pay-later services through a gateway of your choice. The template ships with UI only; connect your preferred provider.

One coupon can be applied per order. Try the demo codes NEXORA10 (10% off), FUTURE25 ($25 off orders over $200) or FREESHIP (free standard shipping).

Orders can be changed or cancelled within one hour of purchase by contacting our support team with your order number.

{{icon:truck}}Shipping & delivery

Standard delivery (3–5 business days) is free on orders over $150, otherwise $12.00. Express delivery (1–2 business days) costs $24.00.

Yes — we ship to more than 60 countries from fulfilment centres in Reno, Rotterdam and Singapore. Duties are calculated at checkout.

As soon as your order ships you receive an email with a tracking link. You can also find tracking details in your account.

{{icon:rotate-ccw}}Returns & warranty

You can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Returns are free with our prepaid label — just keep the original packaging.

Every device includes a 2-year Nexora Care warranty covering manufacturing defects and battery degradation below 80%, with advance replacement.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3–5 business days after we receive your return.

{{icon:lock}}Privacy & account

In this template your cart, wishlist and coupon are stored in your browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.

A production store should publish a privacy policy describing data collection, processing and retention. Health data from wearables is encrypted end-to-end.
